ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built for a better campus life.

CampusConnect is a front-end Web Technology project that brings college events into one clean digital experience.

THE IDEA

Less searching. More doing.

Students often miss great events because information is scattered across notices, groups and social media. CampusConnect presents events in a simple, searchable and mobile-friendly portal.
